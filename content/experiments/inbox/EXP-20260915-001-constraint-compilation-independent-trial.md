# Independent governed trial of Constraint Compilation

## Hypothesis

An independent practitioner who did not author the technique can use the canonical Constraint Compilation card to produce an outcome contract in which every constraint traces to supplied evidence, no preference is upgraded into authority, and an authorized reviewer can accept or reject the result against the documented contract.

## Conditions

- Select one bounded real workflow whose source packet can be retained safely or represented through approved redactions.
- Name a practitioner who did not author `AI-DISCOVER-001`.
- Name an authorized reviewer before execution.
- Record the exact technique revision, model, tools, applicable instructions, and meaningful configuration without secrets.
- Keep planning, execution, provider acknowledgement, human acceptance, and observed outcomes separate.

## Method

1. Freeze or fingerprint the safe source packet and canonical technique revision.
2. Give the independent practitioner the source packet and `AI-DISCOVER-001` without undocumented coaching.
3. Have the practitioner produce the technique's complete output contract.
4. Trace every output constraint to its supplied source and classify unsupported additions.
5. Test the result against one plausible failure scenario and record surprises.
6. Ask the authorized reviewer to accept or reject the result against the card's output and verification contracts.
7. Retain the disposition, failures, and any recommended card revision without automatically changing technique status.

## Results

Not run. No practitioner, reviewer, produced contract, acceptance, repeatability, or outcome is claimed.

## Verification

- Every compiled constraint must trace to the frozen source packet.
- No implementation preference may be presented as authority.
- Material missing context must remain unresolved rather than fabricated.
- Implemented, executed, provider-acknowledged, accepted, and outcome-proven states must remain distinct.
- The human disposition must identify the reviewed artifact and acceptance criteria.

## Failures and surprises

None observed because the experiment has not run. Missing practitioner and reviewer assignments remain explicit prerequisites.

## Decision

Proposed and structurally ready for assignment. Do not promote, relabel, or claim evidence for `AI-DISCOVER-001` until the method is executed and the human disposition is retained.

## Human authority

Repository maintainers may refine the protocol. A named source owner must approve retained or redacted inputs; the practitioner controls their submitted work; an authorized reviewer accepts or rejects the result; lifecycle promotion remains a separate human decision.
